A team design document for a bank transaction anti-fraud system, prepared for Reliable ML / Data Fest Online 2023.

I contributed alongside Sergey Artiukhin, Artem Savelyev, and Eduard Poliakov. This is a collaborative system-design artifact rather than a claim of sole authorship or a deployed production system.
